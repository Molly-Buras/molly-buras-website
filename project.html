My Professional Journey

Development, Work Ethic, and Aspirations in Information Systems

LSU Information Systems Class of May 2027 ISDS 4125 Portfolio Project

This portfolio project is a candid reflection on my progression as an Information Systems student at Louisiana State University, the practical skills I have acquired through coursework and employment, and my clear ambition to build a rewarding professional career.

Academic Foundation at LSU

My journey in higher education at Louisiana State University centers on Information Systems. I chose this major because I have always loved working with people and wanted to understand how technology and organized systems enable organizations to function effectively.

As a senior undergraduate preparing for graduation in May 2027, my coursework in the E. J. Ourso College of Business has given me insight into the analysis and design of information systems (ISDS 4125), data structures, and the importance of alignment between business processes and technical tools.

Developing My Technical Toolkit

Throughout my academic career, I have focused on building hands-on competency in essential tools used across business and technical domains:

  • Microsoft Excel: Utilizing spreadsheets for data analysis, formula calculations, structured reporting, and information organization.
  • SQL: Writing database queries, managing relational data structures, and understanding how data is organized, filtered, and retrieved.
  • Python: Learning foundational programming principles, algorithmic logic, and automated data processing techniques.
  • HTML/CSS: Building structured, semantic web layouts and writing clean, accessible styling for multi-device responsiveness.

These technical competencies serve as the practical foundation for evaluating, analyzing, and improving information workflows.

Real-World Work Ethic & Leadership

My development is deeply informed by continuous, hands-on employment throughout my college years. Rather than viewing employment as separate from my studies, my time in high-volume restaurant environments has forged the core professional skills I bring to any organization.

Growth & Loyalty at Izzo's Illegal Burritos

May 2021 – June 2024 • Over 3 Years of Service

Over three continuous years at Izzo's Illegal Burritos, I advanced from dishwasher to food roller, and ultimately earned promotion to manager. This trajectory demonstrates my core values: loyalty, dependability, and willingness to work hard at every level. As a manager, I led shifts, supervised daily operations, mentored colleagues, and developed strong leadership and crisis-resolution abilities.

Customer Connection at Mike Anderson's

July 2024 – Present • Bartender and Server

At Mike Anderson's, serving and bartending in a fast-paced setting has sharpened my customer service instincts and verbal communication. Successful shifts require constant teamwork with kitchen staff and fellow servers, active listening with guests, and maintaining grace and accuracy under intense dinner rushes.

Why Customer Service Matters in Information Systems

Information systems are built for people. Having years of direct experience listening to customers, managing expectations, and troubleshooting issues on the fly translates directly into being an empathetic, communicative, and solutions-oriented systems professional.

My Personal Principles

In both personal and professional settings, I hold myself accountable to four guiding principles:

  • Hardworking: I do not shy away from demanding tasks or starting from the ground up to achieve mastery.
  • Dedicated: I remain committed to completing obligations with care, diligence, and thoroughness.
  • Honest: I believe transparent, open communication is the bedrock of strong team relationships and system design.
  • Loyal: I invest deeply in the organizations and teams I join, as demonstrated by my multi-year tenure with past employers.

Career Goal: Building Professional Experience

My primary career goal is to transition my academic preparation and interpersonal work experience into professional roles within the Information Systems industry. I am eager to contribute to an organization where I can learn from experienced professionals, solve real business problems, and continue developing my career.

Whether assisting with business analysis, database administration, system design, or technology implementation, I bring genuine enthusiasm for teamwork, strong technical aptitude, and an unwavering work ethic.
